## 3. Fastlane / App Store Connect Upload Instructions

### Option A: App Store Connect Web Drag & Drop (Fastest)
1. Unzip or open `AuthenticHadith_AppStore_Screenshots_Bundle.zip`.
2. In **App Store Connect** > **Authentic Hadith** > **iOS App** > **Version 1.1.3** > **App Previews and Screenshots**.
3. Under **6.7" Display**, drag and drop all 6 files from `iphone67/` (`01_home.png` through `06_sunnah.png`).
4. (Optional) Under **6.9" Display**, drag and drop all 6 files from `iphone69/`.
5. Click **Save**.
